# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful.

This is a curated list of **live Grok Bot templates**: public `https://x.ai/bot/...` share links. It is not a prompt dump and not a Grok Imagine image-template gallery.

## Add a template

1. Confirm the share page still opens and is meant to be public.
2. Open a pull request that adds **one** entry under the right section in `README.md`.
3. Use this shape:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://x.ai/bot/SLUG) - One-line job. By Author.
   ```

4. The description starts with a capital letter and ends with a period.
5. Do not duplicate an existing slug.
6. Do not list unpublished, private, or already-404 links.
7. Do not list Grok Imagine *image* templates.

## What we look for

A named job. An approval boundary when the Bot can send, spend, delete, or publish. One Bot, one job.

## Safety

Shared Bots are third-party. Adding one copies a configuration onto your account. Every Bot on an account shares one computer. Inspect before you add. Keep send, spend, publish, and delete behind your approval.
